FIELD GUIDE

A small problem. An inspectable solution.

Start with the greedy trap

One long contact offers 100 utility. Two compatible shorter contacts offer 120. Compare exact and greedy plans to see why local choices can miss a better allocation.

What is being optimized?

Every request is optional and delivered in full. Maximize data or priority × data; then minimize energy, occupied station ticks, and finally lexical option IDs. Exact search covers only the supplied candidates.

Read the constraints

Stations reserve guard time on both sides. Spacecraft reserve transmission only. Windows are half-open: an interval ending where another starts does not overlap. Blackouts, deadlines, bands and budgets must all hold.

Privacy & limits

Everything runs on your device. No telemetry, ads, accounts, remote services or live spacecraft commands. Only controls are saved if you opt in. CLI imports are capped at 9 requests / 18 windows; real mission operations are out of scope.
